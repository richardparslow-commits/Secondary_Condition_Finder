Specular lobe is drawn around the reflection angle, not the window. The glint is brightest where source→surface→lens are coplanar within the lobe. Move the flag to kill the lobe edge, not the window — flag position is measured from the window, not the lens.

Crew set checklist

1. Sight the window to the subject at the bearing above.
2. Place the flag on the window side, between window and subject, so the shadow line crosses the lobe edge you want to lose.
3. Confirm the camera axis is not coplanar with the source ray — if it is, the lobe blows the frame.
4. Shoot a test of the reflected surface; the glint should sit inside the drawn lobe, not spill past it.
