## Key limitations preserved

- Asthma→OSA: overall pooled association was imprecise and crossed the null; the stronger moderate-to-severe asthma signal was limited and very imprecise. The live proposition was narrowed before verification.
- Barrett's→cancer: limited to esophageal adenocarcinoma rather than all esophageal-cancer histologies, and progression is not treated as inevitable.
- Cardiomyopathy→heart failure: phenotype, ventricular function, chronology, and competing etiologies remain required.
- CES→ED: the reviewed evidence is drawn from surgically treated, predominantly lumbar-disc-herniation/CES cohorts and is not generalized automatically to every CES etiology. The live proposition was narrowed before verification.
- Chronic liver disease→cirrhosis: requires an etiology-specific chronic liver disease and objective fibrosis/progression evidence rather than the umbrella label alone.
