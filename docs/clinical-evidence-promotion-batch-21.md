## Multiple sclerosis → depressive disorder

- Published as association/comorbidity research only.
- A systematic review/meta-analysis of 58 articles including 87,756 patients with MS estimated pooled depression prevalence at 30.5%.
- A later meta-analysis similarly found substantial depression prevalence across MS phenotypes, reinforcing the comorbidity signal while also showing subgroup and measurement heterogeneity.
- No causal pathway metadata is authored. Individual chronology, disability, fatigue, pain, sleep, cognition, treatment, psychosocial factors, and pre-existing psychiatric history require review.
- The clinical source remains `verified: false` pending qualified-human verification.
