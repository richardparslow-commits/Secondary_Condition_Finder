## Cauda equina syndrome → erectile dysfunction

- `cauda-equina-syndrome` is promoted from draft with a single `erectile-dysfunction` relationship.
- A 2025 systematic review of sexual dysfunction after lumbar disc herniation and cauda equina syndrome identified erectile dysfunction as the most frequently reported male sexual disorder in the included literature and documented persistent sexual problems after CES.
- A 2026 systematic review of long-term CES outcomes found sexual dysfunction common and persistent, with a pooled estimate of 58.4%; limited evidence associated worse outcomes with markers of sacral nerve-root involvement.
- The repository deliberately does **not** add causal pathway metadata. The relationship is published as condition-specific complication/outcome research, not as an automatic individual nexus.
- Development safeguards require a documented ED diagnosis, CES chronology and sacral neurologic findings, pre-CES sexual-function history where available, evaluation of vascular/endocrine/medication/psychogenic and other competing causes, and a qualified individual medical opinion when a secondary theory is asserted.
