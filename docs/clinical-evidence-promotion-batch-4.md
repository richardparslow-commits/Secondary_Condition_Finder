# Clinical Evidence Promotion Batch 4

Batch 4 preserves the exact **300 catalog / 300 knowledge-base** integrity contract.

## Expansion promotions

- `acl-injury` → `osteoarthritis`: published as affected-knee post-traumatic research using the ACL injury/knee OA umbrella systematic review and meta-analysis (PMID 33852440). The mapping requires same-knee chronology, imaging, associated meniscal/cartilage injury, treatment/instability history, and competing OA risk review.
- `pcl-injury` → `osteoarthritis`: published cautiously using the isolated-PCL systematic review (PMID 34505176), with explicit acknowledgement that much of the included evidence was observational and that individual review must address the injured knee, instability, associated joint injury, treatment, and other OA risks.

## Legacy evidence repairs

- `anosmia` → `depressive-disorder`: replaces legal-only support with the 2026 systematic review/meta-analysis of olfactory impairment and depression (PMID 42424043). The mapping states association, not direction or individual causation.
- `alopecia-areata` → `depressive-disorder`: replaces legal-only support with a systematic review/meta-analysis distinguishing depressive disorders and symptoms in alopecia areata (PMID 36696123). The mapping remains association-focused and requires chronology/psychosocial review.

## Safety withdrawal

- `agranulocytosis`: returned to draft. Its depression and GERD suggestions used only 38 CFR 3.310 and generic chronic-condition/treatment language rather than agranulocytosis-specific clinical evidence.
