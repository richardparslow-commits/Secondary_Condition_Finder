The focused set is: carpal tunnel syndrome, chronic fatigue syndrome, insomnia, hypothyroidism, illness anxiety disorder, median neuropathy, micro TBI, migraine headaches, mild TBI, narcolepsy, peripheral neuropathy, radial neuropathy, REM sleep disorder, and social anxiety disorder.

Two new relationships meet the bounded publication threshold:

- `insomnia` → `depressive-disorder`, based on prospective incident-depression evidence and limited to cases with an independently established insomnia diagnosis, compatible chronology, and individual medical assessment;
- `migraine-headaches` → `depressive-disorder`, using a somatopsychic pathway and prospective incident-depression evidence while explicitly preserving population-level, demographic, bidirectionality, and competing-cause limits.

## Safety rule for safe drafts

A safe draft is an acceptable reviewed product state. The objective is not to publish every identity regardless of evidence. A relationship remains draft when direction, phenotype, mechanism, target identity, treatment exposure, source quality, diagnostic overlap, or the application's available intake fields cannot safely enforce the proposition.

## Completion definition

A clinical evidence batch is repository-controlled only when the KB and documentation agree, citation governance is clean, exact-head CI and Vercel Agent immutable-head review are clean on the same SHA, and the exact merge SHA is independently proven by `main` CI and Production Runtime Readiness.

Any head change invalidates the prior exact-head CI and Vercel Agent review and requires both gates again on the new head.
