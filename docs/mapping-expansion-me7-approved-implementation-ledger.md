### 1. `benign-prostatic-hyperplasia -> urethral-stricture`
- Existing P1 target required before implementation: `chronic-kidney-disease`
- Phenotype restriction: **treatment-related urethral stricture after TURP or HoLEP for BPH**
- PMID **37561537**
- DOI `10.1177/03000605231190763`
- Citation: Chen F, Chen Y, Zou Y, Wang Y, Wu X, Chen M. Comparison of holmium laser enucleation and transurethral resection of prostate in benign prostatic hyperplasia: a systematic review and meta-analysis. J Int Med Res. 2023;51(8):3000605231190763. PMID 37561537. doi:10.1177/03000605231190763.
- Evidence type: systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials
- Approved proposition: Treatment-mediated urethral-stricture risk after TURP or HoLEP performed for documented benign prostatic hyperplasia.
- Approved publication guard: Require documented benign prostatic hyperplasia, documented transurethral resection of the prostate (TURP) or holmium laser enucleation of the prostate (HoLEP), and a separately established urethral stricture arising after that procedure with compatible chronology. Do not infer urethral stricture from untreated BPH, lower urinary tract symptoms, or bladder-outlet obstruction alone. Preserve prior urethral instrumentation or trauma, infection, lichen sclerosus, prior pelvic surgery or radiation, and other recognized causes of urethral stricture.
