# Clinical Evidence Promotion Batch 22

Batch 22 preserves the exact **300 catalog / 300 knowledge-base** contract while removing the largest remaining legal/rating-only neurological mapping, clinically repairing post-concussion headache research, and promoting one Wave-1 seizure-disorder relationship.

## Safety withdrawal: broad skull-fracture mapping

`skull-fracture-non-basilar-and-basal` is returned to draft. The prior mapping published six distinct downstream diagnoses—migraine, post-concussion syndrome, tinnitus, vestibular disorder, depressive disorder, and anosmia—using only rating/legal authorities. The grouped identity spans heterogeneous fracture patterns, including basal and non-basilar injury, so broad residual literature cannot safely establish all six diagnosis-specific relationships. Future publication requires fracture-subtype- and outcome-specific clinical evidence.

## Post-concussion syndrome → migraine-headaches repair

- The prior anxiety edge is removed.
- The headache edge is retained only after replacing §3.310 with a 2026 clinical review of persistent post-traumatic headache after concussion/mild TBI.
- Persistent post-traumatic headache is not treated as synonymous with primary migraine. The catalog target is used only when a clinician has diagnosed migraine or persistent post-traumatic headache with a migraine/probable-migraine phenotype and has documented the temporal relationship to the concussion.
- No causal pathway metadata is added from phenotype overlap alone.

## Seizure disorder → depressive disorder

- `seizure-disorder` → `depressive-disorder` is promoted as association/comorbidity research only.
- A 2026 systematic review/meta-analysis of adults with epilepsy found a pooled depressive-symptom proportion of 40% in the cross-sectional depression meta-analysis, with substantial heterogeneity.
- The mapping requires review of seizure phenotype/control, antiseizure medications, chronology, sleep, cognition, psychosocial factors, and independent psychiatric causes.
- The clinical source remains `verified: false` pending qualified-human verification.
