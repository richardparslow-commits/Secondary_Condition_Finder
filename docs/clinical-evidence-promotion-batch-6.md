# Clinical Evidence Promotion Batch 6

Batch 6 preserves the exact **300 catalog / 300 knowledge-base** integrity contract while reducing legacy citation debt.

## Expansion promotion

- `hyperaldosteronism` → `hypertension`: published only for a **confirmed primary-aldosteronism phenotype**. The 2025 Endocrine Society Clinical Practice Guideline describes primary aldosteronism as a common cause of hypertension and explains the mechanism from autonomous aldosterone excess through renal sodium retention and volume expansion to elevated blood pressure. The mapping therefore uses a `direct-secondary` pathway but explicitly requires aldosterone/renin evidence, blood-pressure chronology, treatment history, and competing-cause review. The broader catalog label must not be treated as proof that every unspecified hyperaldosteronism or endocrine-neoplasm presentation follows this physiology.

## Safety withdrawal

- `addisons-disease`: returned to draft. Its three published suggestions—peripheral neuropathy, diabetic retinopathy, and chronic kidney disease—used only rating-schedule authorities and generic endocrine/metabolic language rather than Addison's-disease-specific clinical evidence.

## Bounded identity/modeling cleanup

- `hyperpituitarism`: the stable catalog ID remains unchanged, but the display name is narrowed to **Hyperpituitarism (Prolactin-Secreting Pituitary Dysfunction)** to match the express scope of 38 CFR 4.119 / DC 7916. Existing aliases and `verified: false` rating-reference state are preserved. This prevents the catalog identity from reading as a generic umbrella for every form of pituitary hormone excess.
