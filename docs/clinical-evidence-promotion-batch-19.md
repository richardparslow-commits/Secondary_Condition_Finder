# Clinical Evidence Promotion Batch 19

Batch 19 preserves the exact **300 catalog / 300 knowledge-base** contract while removing four more published legal/rating-only mental-health mappings and promoting one phenotype-bounded endocrine progression relationship.

## Safety withdrawals

The following mappings are returned to draft:

- `bipolar-disorder`;
- `persistent-depressive-disorder`;
- `social-anxiety-disorder`;
- `somatic-symptom-disorder`.

Each had the same three generic published propositions—sleep apnea, GERD, and migraine—with only §3.310 or the migraine rating schedule attached. Those authorities do not establish the medical relationships. The withdrawal does not assert that an individual relationship is impossible; it means the repository will not publish those directions without condition-specific clinical evidence.

## Promotion: autoimmune thyroiditis → hypothyroidism

- `thyroiditis` → `hypothyroidism` is published only for chronic autoimmune/Hashimoto-type thyroiditis or a clinically comparable persistent thyroid-autoimmunity phenotype.
- A 2026 multicenter cohort followed 669 initially euthyroid patients with positive thyroid autoantibodies; 155 progressed to overt or subclinical hypothyroidism during a median three-year follow-up, and baseline TSH, FT4, and TPOAb were independent progression factors.
- The mapping therefore carries bounded `disease-progression` metadata and requires documented autoimmune phenotype, longitudinal TSH/FT4, chronology, and competing-cause review.
- It expressly does not generalize the pathway to subacute, painless, postpartum, infectious, or other transient thyroiditis syndromes without evidence of persistent hypothyroidism.
- The new clinical source remains `verified: false` pending qualified-human verification.
