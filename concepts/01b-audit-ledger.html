Concept 1b — The Audit

Ledger-forward frame of Concept 1. Same set, same props, different camera and inverted light logic: one narrow hard shaft now rakes the hand-tooled leather and makes the Lone Star read in relief, while the tablet becomes a cold glowing slab out of focus behind it. 1600×1000. Complements the master frame — it does not replace it.

What changes from the master

CameraMoved in and down. The ledger is cropped by the bottom-left and right edges and fills the near plane; the machine retreats to mid-distance at 4.6 px of blur.
Light directionReversed. The sun rakes from camera-left at a low angle instead of camera-right, so every shadow in this frame falls the opposite way to the master's.
Legible copyNow the tooling, not the screen: UNDERWRITING AUDIT LEDGER, EXHIBIT A, the NDC and rule number. The alert strings are still on the screen but deliberately unreadable at this focus distance.
Red disciplineOne crimson event remains — the alert panel, blurred — plus the ledger's ribbon. Nothing else in frame is red.

Shot notes

Optics100mm macro at f/2.8, sensor plane roughly parallel to the leather so the tooling stays readable across the plane; the tablet sits ~1.2m behind the focal plane.
LightOne hard source at 12–15° above the leather and almost edge-on (raked), which is what makes an embossed impression read as relief — frontal light would flatten it. Silver card far left for a 3:1 fill; no soft light anywhere near the leather.
Machines in frameKeep the screen at 90–95% so it blooms slightly at this aperture. The lens should turn the red panel into a shape, not a message — that is the whole point of this frame.
Focus stackStack the leather (three planes: nearest stitching 1–2cm closer than the star) and leave the pen as the only element flat in focus. Do not stack the tablet.
Text safetySame rule as the master, inverted: the legible copy here is the tooling — UNDERWRITING AUDIT LEDGER, EXHIBIT A and the NDC line — so version-control those, and keep the on-screen regulatory wording either absent or unreadable. See the prompt pack.
Pairs withMaster frame 1 as the "verdict lands" beat; this frame as "the human answers it". Slide sequence: 1 then 1b, same crop ratio, so the eye stays in the same place.
