Concept 1c — Reflected

Third frame of Concept 1. Extreme close-up raked across the tablet's glossy panel so the Texas flag and the window are mirrored in the glass — the brief's stated contrast made literal: woven red against emissive red, on one surface. Same set, same props, same light as the master. 1600×1000.

What this frame does that the others cannot

The two reds touchThe flag's reflected red stripe runs directly above the alert's crimson banner. Earlier frames contrasted red with navy and white at a distance; this one puts the two reds in physical contact and lets the eye discover they are different materials — one woven and sun-warmed, one emissive and cold-edged.
Texas inside the machineRaking the camera close enough to catch a reflection means the state appears on the screen, not behind it. The trap contains the thing it is deciding about.
Still a close-upNo deviation from the brief. Shallow depth of field is doing the storytelling: the notebook edge and brass pen are the near, defocused frame; the screen is the only sharp plane; the wall and flag fall off behind.
Fidelity keptBoth regulated lines stay fully legible through the glass, the portal UI is complete, the ledger crosses the device's near edge and the pen lies in the spine — now in the near foreground where the raking angle puts them.

Shot notes

Optics60mm macro at f/2.8, camera about 25cm from the panel and raked down the screen at roughly 12° off the glass. The reflection only appears once the lens axis is low enough that the flag falls inside the screen's specular lobe — so the angle is a lighting decision, not just a framing one.
LightFlag on the wall lit so it is bright enough to reflect — a mirror shows only what is lit. Keep the flag two stops hotter than feels natural on set; the panel will take most of that away. One hard source, same raking direction as the master, plus a silver card to lift the crimson out of the glass.
The trap in the setupPolariser off. Any polarising filter on the lens kills the reflection you are here for. If you must cut screen glare for the copy, cut it with the screen's own brightness and a black flag card behind the camera, not with a filter.
FocusFocus on the word INELIGIBLE, then stop down until the panel's near corner is just sharp. Deliberately leave the notebook edge and the pen outside the plane — they should read as material, not as objects.
Text safetyWording identical to the master and still composited in post. The reflection sits over the alert, so grade the reflection pass below the type in the stack or the copy will lose contrast against the reflected canvas white.
Pairs with1 (verdict lands, machine dominant) → 1b (the human answers) → 1c (the machine contains the state). Use 1c as the closing frame of the three.
